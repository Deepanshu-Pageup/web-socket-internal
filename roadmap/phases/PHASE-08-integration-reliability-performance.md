# Phase 08 — Integration, Reliability & Performance

**Status:** Not started  
**Depends on:** Phases 03–07

## Objective

Validate the server with at least one real company project and agreed workload.

## Scope

- Integrate one representative frontend and backend.
- Reconnect and resubscribe behavior.
- Disconnect and cleanup tests.
- Slow-client/backpressure tests.
- Payload and connection-limit tests.
- Concurrency and load tests.
- Graceful shutdown and restart behavior.
- Security review.
- Measure memory, CPU, throughput, and delivery latency.

## Deliverables

- Integration report.
- Load-test report.
- Known limitations and tuning guidance.
- Release acceptance checklist.

## Exit criteria

Agreed workload and reliability/security acceptance criteria pass. Do not claim capacity without measured results on documented hardware.
